# knowledge

What the engine has found on your own sites, in fixed templates. Private to you; never shared.

- `index.md`: one line per note. Read first; rebuilt with `python tools/build_index.py`.
- `councils/<council>.md`: one per council (plan, supply, assessment documents, behaviour seen in decisions and appeals).
- `sites/<TITLE>.md`: one per Land Registry title (or enquiry), with status, findings, verdict, actions and open items.
- `agents/<name>.md`: one per agent, promoter, developer or consultant met.
- `contacts/log.md`: every letter, email and call.
- `learned-checks.md`: checks the engine learned on your sites, added to the coverage checklist.
- `open-items.md`: unresolved checks by title.

Nothing else is written here: no chat transcripts, no narrative. Method learned on a site goes into the skills.
