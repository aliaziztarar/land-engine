# Coverage checklist

Every site review ends with every line below marked **checked** (finding and source), **not relevant** (reason) or **could not check** (what was tried, why it failed, what the user can do). Lines learned on the user's own sites are in `knowledge/learned-checks.md` and are checked too. SearchLand's panel or report covers many of these; where it shows the item clearly, a light confirmation from the official source is enough; where anything is flagged or unclear, research it fully (site-review step 3).

## Title and ownership
- Registered owners, addresses, entry dates, price paid.
- Notices, restrictions and charges in favour of developers, promoters or lenders.
- Restrictive covenants; overage or clawback in transfers (filed documents).
- Easements and rights over and for the land; utility deeds.
- Leases and occupiers; business tenancies; agricultural tenancies (Agricultural Holdings Act or farm business tenancy).
- Deceased owners, executors, trusts, dissolved companies.
- Mines and minerals excepted from the title.
- Unregistered strips between the site and the road.

## Access and highways
- Frontage to an adopted public road; who owns the verge; ransom risk.
- Visibility splays for the road's speed; speed limit; road width; footway to the village.
- Public rights of way across or beside the site.
- Bus routes and frequency; walking distance to school, shop, GP; nearest station.
- Highway authority comments on nearby applications.

## Flood and water
- Flood zones 2 and 3 (river and sea); functional flood plain.
- Surface water flood risk (1 in 30, 1 in 100, 1 in 1000 year).
- Groundwater, reservoir flood risk; source protection zones.
- Drainage route: a secure outfall, infiltration (soil and chalk), sewer capacity.
- Sewage treatment works capacity and any embargo; water company comments.
- Nutrient neutrality catchment; water neutrality zone.

## Heritage and archaeology
- Listed buildings on or near the site, and their setting.
- Conservation area (in, adjoining or in view) and its appraisal.
- Scheduled monuments; registered parks and gardens; battlefields.
- Locally listed heritage assets; non designated heritage assets named in reports.
- Archaeology: the county Historic Environment Record; past finds nearby.

## Landscape and countryside
- National Landscape (formerly AONB) and its setting; National Park.
- Green Belt; grey belt potential.
- Local gaps, strategic gaps, important open areas, local green space.
- Landscape character area and sensitivity rating; capacity studies; protected views.
- Settlement boundary (adopted and emerging); distance from it.
- Agricultural land grade (best and most versatile is grades 1, 2 and 3a).

## Ecology and trees
- SSSI, SAC, SPA, Ramsar, and their impact risk zones; recreation mitigation payments (SAMM).
- Local wildlife sites (SINC); ancient woodland and its buffer; veteran trees.
- Tree preservation orders; hedgerows (Hedgerow Regulations).
- Protected species likely (bats, great crested newts, badgers, dormice, birds).
- Biodiversity net gain baseline and whether 10 percent is achievable on site.

## Ground and environment
- Contamination (past uses, landfill within 250 m).
- Unexploded ordnance risk; mining and subsidence (Coal Authority); radon.
- Caves, solution features, unstable ground.
- Minerals safeguarding areas; waste sites nearby.
- Noise (roads, railways, airfields, businesses: agent of change); odour (farms, sewage works); air quality management areas.
- High pressure gas pipelines and their consultation zones; overhead power lines; pylons.
- Airport and military safeguarding zones; wind turbine zones.

## Services and infrastructure
- School places (primary and secondary at capacity or not); GP capacity.
- Utilities: electricity capacity, gas, water, broadband.
- Infrastructure projects planned nearby (roads, rail, reservoirs, energy) that help or block.

## Policy and planning
- Local plan stage; allocations near the site; settlement tier.
- Five year housing land supply and Housing Delivery Test (live figures).
- Land availability assessment entries in every round; call for sites.
- Neighbourhood plan (made or emerging) and its policies for this land.
- Article 4 directions; permitted development removed.
- Affordable housing, mix, density, space standards, self build policies.
- CIL; typical S106 items.
- Planning history of the site and neighbours; appeals.
- Precedent: two examples only, one approved and one refused, most recent first and within 10 years, for a similar scheme near the site (or the nearest equivalent in the council area).
- Prematurity risk against an emerging allocation.

## Market and people
- Listed for sale now or in the past; auction lots; recent sale price.
- Promoters, housebuilders or consultants publicly working the site.
- Local opposition groups; parish council stance in minutes and consultation responses.
- Owner circumstances that matter (lives on the land, income from it, age, family).
