# Landowner call rules (binding)

- Teleprompter format: numbered headings; the exact words to say in quoted blocks; Claude's notes (for example "wait for yes", "silence") in italics on their own lines, never mixed into the spoken line, so the script can be read live.
- BuildUp's 30/70 rule: the user speaks about 30 percent, the owner 70. Short open questions, then silence; two seconds of silence after every answer; no yes or no questions except on ownership.
- Hear their story first; say what we found only after.
- Never on a call: a figure, home numbers, tilted balance, HDT, supply years, what the assessment "promises" ("the council's position is helpful" is the limit), nutrient neutrality, our view of a clause as a problem, Street View observations, and nothing that turns it into a negotiation.
- If asked for a price: "I never put a number on a first call, it wouldn't be fair to either of us. The next step is for me to go through it properly with our planning team and come back to you with what's realistically possible and a ballpark figure. Would you be open to that?"
- Pushbacks: "What are you going to pay?" (a number today would be a guess, and I don't want to guess with your land); "I've got other people looking at it" (of course, you should; let me come back with ours and you can compare); a clause they raise (it affects what the land is worth to both of us, so we'll work it into the figure; that's why I'd like to see the wording).
- Always ask: ownership (name alone, company, family, lender), what is signed with anyone, what they want (sell as is, sell with permission, develop with someone), timing drivers, whether the land reaches the public road without crossing anyone else's, the paperwork they hold (legal pack, drawings, council letters).
- Close with the next step and a date ("within the week" or "within three weeks"), the email address, and the best time to reach them; then stop and let them end the call.
- Before dialling: the site note open, the SearchLand map, flood map and assessment entry to hand; ring from the business number in the profile.
- Missed call: brief email saying you tried, call again next day at a different time; findings stay off email.
- When the site does not fit the model (small urban plot, long refusal history), say so plainly on the call and offer the decline email.
