# Owner with a developer notice on the title (invented example)

Subject: Land at the corner of Station Road and Long Lane, Ashcombe

Please excuse the direct approach.

My name is {short_name} and I'm {role} at {company}, {company_description}. I'm writing about your land at the corner of Station Road and Long Lane, on the southern edge of Ashcombe. We've looked at it closely, and it's a site we'd very much like to take on. We have an active need for land in this part of {county}, and your land meets what we're looking for.

I'm aware there is a notice on your title for an option agreement dated 1 August 2019 with Example Homes Limited. Agreements like that are often for a fixed number of years, and after this long it may have run its course, be coming to an end, or simply have gone quiet. If any of those is the case, I'd like to talk about taking the land forward ourselves, on terms where you keep your land and owe nothing if planning isn't granted. I'm not asking you to do anything that cuts across an agreement that is still in force.

I understand this comes with a lot of questions, and you may well have some hesitations. But rest assured, it's considerably simpler than most people expect, and most owners we speak to are surprised at what can be offered and how the process works.

If you're curious, I'd welcome a conversation. It would be informal, with no obligation on either side, and you'd come away knowing exactly what the options are. If the timing isn't right, I'd be grateful if you'd keep this letter.

Please reach out to me directly on {mobile}, or by email at {email}.

Thank you for reading this, and I look forward to hearing from you.
