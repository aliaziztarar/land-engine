# Executor (invented example: an estate with a housebuilder's agreement on part of the farm)

Subject: Land at Brook Farm, Ashcombe

Please excuse the direct approach.

My name is {short_name} and I'm {role} at {company}, {company_description}. I'm writing to you as executor of the late Mrs Mary Brook, about the land at Brook Farm in Ashcombe. We've looked at it closely, and we have an active need for land in this part of {county}.

I'm aware the estate entered into an agreement with Example Homes Limited dated 20 June 2025, which affects part of the farm, and I'm not asking you to do anything that cuts across it. What I'd like to explore is whether we could work together, as a joint venture or on whatever basis suits the estate, on the land outside that agreement, or alongside it in a way that adds value for the beneficiaries.

I understand this comes with a lot of questions, and you may well have some hesitations. But rest assured, it's considerably simpler than most people expect, and we pay for the planning work ourselves.

If you're open to it, I'd welcome a conversation. It would be informal, with no obligation on either side, and you'd come away knowing exactly what the options are. If the timing isn't right, I'd be grateful if you'd keep this letter.

Please reach out to me directly on {mobile}, or by email at {email}.

# Trustees (invented example: pension scheme trustees, one field of a larger holding)

We've looked at it closely, and it's a site we'd very much like to take on. We have an active need for land in this part of {county}, and your field meets what we're looking for.

To be clear, it's only this one field of about 21 acres I'm writing about, not the rest of your land at Ashcombe.
