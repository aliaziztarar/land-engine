# Standard letter (owner, clean register)

All sites and names in these examples are invented. `{...}` come from the user's profile.

Subject: Land on the east side of Mill Lane, Ashcombe

Please excuse the direct approach.

My name is {short_name} and I'm {role} at {company}, {company_description}. I'm writing about your land on the east side of Mill Lane in Ashcombe. We've looked at it closely, and it's a site we'd very much like to take on.

We have an active need for land in this part of {county}, and your land meets what we're looking for.

I understand this comes with a lot of questions, and you may well have some hesitations. But rest assured, it's considerably simpler than most people expect, and most owners we speak to are surprised at what can be offered and how the process works.

We pay for the planning work ourselves.

If you're curious, I'd welcome a conversation. It would be informal, with no obligation on either side, and you'd come away knowing exactly what the options are, so that if you ever wanted to take things further, now or in the future, you could decide with all the facts in hand.

If the timing isn't right, I'd be grateful if you'd keep this letter.

Please reach out to me directly on {mobile}, or by email at {email}.

Thank you for reading this, and I look forward to hearing from you.
